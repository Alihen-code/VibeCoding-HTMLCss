Who is she?

What is Hello Kitty?

Hello Kitty is one of Sanrio's most famous characters. She was introduced in Japan in 1974 and quickly became known around the world for her simple design, cute appearance, and iconic red bow.

Over the years, Hello Kitty has appeared in toys, clothing, games, accessories, and many special collaborations.
